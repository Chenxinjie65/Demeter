# Security Policy

Demeter V2 has completed its repository-local engineering gates but has not
completed the independent audit, production-chain validation, or operational
approval required to hold production assets.

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Chenxinjie65/Demeter/security/advisories/new).
Do not disclose an unpatched vulnerability in a public issue.

Include the affected contract and function, impact, assumptions, reproduction
steps, and a minimal proof of concept when possible. The maintainers will
acknowledge the report, assess severity, and coordinate remediation and
disclosure. This policy does not promise a bounty or response deadline.
